# Dashboard Guide

- Overview: executive snapshot and review queue.
- Risk Matrix: score relationships and area comparison.
- Electrical Systems: component-level risk review and workshop radar.
- Incidents & Load: incident and load patterns.
- Workshop Profiles: filtered records.
- Priority Queue: score threshold review queue.
- Scenario Lab: before-vs-after what-if analysis.
- Image Review: local generic image-statistics signal.
- Reports & Export: CSV and Markdown outputs.
- Data Explorer: raw records and quality summary.
